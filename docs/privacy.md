# What2Eat 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-08-14

## 中文

What2Eat（家里的菜）是一个完全离线的家庭菜单应用。

- **不收集任何数据**：应用不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人信息或使用数据。
- **本地存储**：你的菜单、自建菜品、照片和偏好设置只保存在你自己设备的本地存储中，卸载应用即被删除。
- **照片权限**：仅当你主动选择"添加照片"时，应用才会调起系统相册选择器；所选照片仅保存在本机，不会上传。
- **无网络通信**：应用本体不发起任何网络请求；点击外部链接（如 GitHub 项目主页）会跳转到系统浏览器。
- **无第三方 SDK**：不包含任何广告、统计或追踪组件。

## English

What2Eat is a fully offline family meal-planning app.

- **No data collection**: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.
- **Local storage only**: your menus, custom dishes, photos, and preferences are stored solely on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
- **Photos**: the system photo picker is invoked only when you actively choose to add a photo; selected photos stay on your device.
- **No network communication**: the app itself makes no network requests; external links open in your system browser.
- **No third-party SDKs**: no ads, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
